# Authority Boundary

## Core invariant

More intelligence or capability does not automatically create more authority.

Systems may be granted bounded autonomous operating permissions within explicitly defined rules and limits. Those permissions do not constitute sovereignty.

## Boundary

Human authority remains outside the systems.

The systems may:

- execute authorized operations
- coordinate permitted capabilities
- run tests
- observe results
- maintain evidence
- identify failures
- propose actions

The systems may not:

- redefine the constitutional boundary
- grant themselves authority
- override human authority
- treat capability as authorization

This repository records the architectural boundary. It does not itself grant authority.
